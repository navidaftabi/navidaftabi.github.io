About

Navid Aftabi is a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Washington, with a minor in Computer Science. His broader research interest spans systems modeling, optimization, and data science, with a focus on the cyber-physical security and resilience of industrial cyber-physical systems (ICPS). He develops data-driven and model-based methods integrating optimization, AI, and high-dimensional statistical learning methods for resilience assessment and design, system diagnosis and anomaly detection, and cyberattack-resilient control. His work appears in IISE Transactions, IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering, and the Journal of Manufacturing Systems. He holds M.Sc. degrees in Industrial Engineering from Sabanci University and in Industrial and Systems Engineering from Sharif University of Technology, and a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from the University of Tabriz.
